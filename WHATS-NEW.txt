NORTHFIELD MILLS: PORTAL + FAVICON UPDATE

IF THE PORTAL SAYS "THE PORTAL'S SERVER ISN'T ON THE LIVE SITE"
- The api function (netlify/functions/api.mjs) wasn't deployed. Netlify → Deploys →
  open the latest deploy → Functions should list: api, access, gmail, gmail-callback,
  submission-created. If api is missing: put your netlify/functions/api.mjs (and
  netlify/lib/) back in the GitHub repo and push. Don't use drag-and-drop.

HOW TO DEPLOY
1. Copy everything in this folder into your GitHub repo, replacing the old files.
2. KEEP your existing netlify/functions/ and netlify/lib/ folders. Do not delete them.
   This zip adds ONE new file to your functions folder: netlify/functions/access.mjs
   (it saves what each staff member can open, and who has signed in). It doesn't
   replace any of your files.
3. Commit and push. Netlify deploys automatically. Don't use drag-and-drop:
   it skips the functions.
4. Sign in to the portal as an admin and open Accounts → System check.
   Every line should be green. A red line says exactly what to fix.

WHAT WAS BROKEN
- Conversations, Email list, Gmail count: one line in the page referred to a stat
  box that no longer existed, so everything after it stopped drawing. Fixed.
- Favicon: assets/favicon.webp was really a JPEG, and 404.html asked for
  "Favicon.webp" (capital F), which Netlify can't find. Replaced with a proper icon
  set at the site root (favicon.ico, icons/, apple-touch-icon.png).

- Tabs jumping back to Overview: the sidebar could be clicked before the portal
  had finished checking who you are, and admin tabs then bounced to Overview.
  If /api/me was slow or failed, it stayed stuck that way. Now the sidebar is
  locked until your access is confirmed. The portal retries up to 3 times, and
  if the server really fails it shows the error with a Try again button.

ACCESS CHANGES
- Staff can now open Conversations (contact form messages).
  NOTE: your /api function decides what data staff receive. If staff see
  Conversations but it's always empty, api.mjs is only sending messages to admins.
  Send me netlify/functions/api.mjs and I'll change that.
- Gmail Inbox and Email list stay admin-only. Staff see them with a lock. Opening
  one shows "You don't have access" with a "Request access from an admin" button.
  The request arrives as a ticket ("Access request: Gmail Inbox") that admins see
  in Tickets. To approve, change the person to Admin in Accounts and resolve the ticket.

ACCESS + STATUS (NEW)
- Admins choose what each staff member can open: Tickets, Conversations, Leads,
  Gmail Inbox, Email list. In Accounts, click "3 of 5 areas" in their row. Changes
  save right away, and staff pick them up within a minute. Admins always have everything.
- Staff asking for access: "Give access" turns on just the area they asked for
  (instead of making them an admin).
- Status is automatic: "Invited" until someone signs in for the first time, then
  "Active". Admins can also set "On leave" or "Deactivated" (which blocks them).
- Reloading the page keeps you in the Team Portal. Going back to the website
  (the "Back to website" link, the logo, or the browser Back button) signs you out,
  so you sign in again next time.
- NOTE: Gmail and the Email list come from your server. If a staff member you gave
  access sees an "only admins" message there, gmail.mjs / api.mjs need a small
  update. Send them to me.

ACCOUNTS PAGE (REDESIGNED)
- Your account is the main thing at the top: name, job title, email, Admin/Staff,
  Change password, Sign out, and what you can open (locked areas let staff ask for access).
- Below it, "Who has access": one list of everyone, with filters (Everyone / Admins /
  Staff / Deactivated) and search. Admins change access and deactivate right in the list.
- Access requests only show up when someone is waiting, inside that list, with
  Approve / Decline. Staff "request access" tickets appear there too: "Make admin"
  upgrades them and closes the ticket.
- System status is a small button at the top (green, amber or red dot) that opens a pop-up.

GMAIL, CONTACTS AND LEADS (NEW)
- Gmail has all of Gmail's folders: Inbox, Starred, Snoozed, Important, Sent,
  Drafts, Scheduled, All mail, Spam, Trash. The Inbox has Gmail's tabs: Primary,
  Promotions, Social, Updates, Forums. There's also an "Unread only" filter.
- Gmail stays in sync on its own. Every 45 seconds, and when you come back to the
  browser tab, new mail appears and mail deleted in Gmail disappears.
- Contacts (button at the top of Gmail): people who emailed you or you wrote to are
  suggested. Newsletters, no-reply addresses and Northfield Mills addresses are
  skipped. Accept reads their email signature and fills in name, job title, company,
  phone and website for you to check. Decline hides them (restore anytime).
  You can also save a contact straight from any open email.
- Leads have a Job title and a Phone field, and both are in the CSV export.
- Each lead has an "Email activity" tab showing your emails with that person.
  New emails are logged in the lead's history automatically:
    their email            -> Response received (sequence paused)
    your first email       -> Initial outreach (Follow-up #1 scheduled)
    later emails, no reply -> Follow-up #1, #2, then Final
    your reply to them     -> Reply sent
  Nothing is ever logged twice, and your hand-logged history is kept.
- Leads and contacts are saved in the browser you use (same as before), so export
  a CSV now and then as a backup.

NOTE FOR YOUR gmail FUNCTION (gmail.mjs)
- Folders and tabs work by passing Gmail search words in "q" (for example
  "in:inbox category:promotions"). Your function already passes q through.
- Spam and Trash also send includeSpamTrash=true. If those two stay empty on the
  live site, gmail.mjs needs to pass that setting on to Google. Send me the file
