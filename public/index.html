We do not knowingly collect sensitive information, and we ask that you not include it in messages to us.

We use information collected through the website:

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site and communicate with you, such as our website host and email service, and only as needed for them to provide those services.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Northfield Mills or others.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Our site may use technologies necessary for the website to operate properly and securely. If we add analytics, advertising cookies, or other tracking technologies in the future, we will update this policy as appropriate.

How long we keep it

We keep your email on our launch list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

We keep contact messages for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping afterward.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email we send, or by writing to us.

You can also ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing info@northfieldmills.com .
